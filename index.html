How to interpret this

UP: aligned trend and momentum, with adequate volume and no risk veto.

DOWN: aligned bearish trend and momentum, with adequate volume and no risk veto.

WAIT: mixed readings, low volume, elevated volatility, or stale feed.

Important: this model does not know the exact Kalshi strike, settlement source, or order-book prices. A 15-minute directional move is not necessarily the same as a specific Kalshi contract outcome.

Validation: do not interpret a heuristic score as win probability. Real confidence requires timestamped out-of-sample backtests, calibration, fees, and slippage analysis.
